ICON PLACEHOLDER
================

This directory should contain white PNG icons (32x32 pixels).

Replace these files with your custom icons:
- 1Icon.png through 10Icon.png (or as needed)

Icon Requirements:
- Format: PNG with transparency
- Size: 32x32 pixels  
- Color: White (#FFFFFF)
- Background: Transparent

Icon Sources:
- Flaticon.com (free icons)
- GIMP (free icon editor)
- Photoshop or Paint.NET

Market Icons Needed:
1Icon.png - London market
2Icon.png - New York market  
3Icon.png - Sydney market
4Icon.png - Tokyo market

For now, the app will work without custom icons.
Droptop will display generic icons or use theme defaults.
